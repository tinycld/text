## Picking up where you left off

Click the **Text** icon in the left rail and Text reopens the last document you had open — your work is one click away from anywhere in the app. The rail remembers per package, so the **Calc** icon does the same for spreadsheets.

If you've never opened a document in this workspace, the Text icon brings you to a small landing panel with three options instead.

## The landing panel

You'll see the panel on a fresh workspace, after the device's storage has been cleared, or when you sign in on a new browser. It offers three ways to start:

- **New document** — creates a blank document and drops you straight into the editor.
- **Upload files** — picks one or more `.docx`, `.md`, or `.txt` files from your device. A single file opens directly; multiple files land in [Drive's recent view](help://drive:getting-started) so you can choose which to open first.
- **Recent files** — sends you to Drive's **Recent** view, where every package's recently-touched files are listed together.

## Resetting the rail

There's no manual "forget" — the rail simply updates whenever you open a different document. Closing the tab or signing out doesn't reset it; opening a new file does.
